## Sound effects (CC0)

- Kenney, "Impact Sounds" (kenney.nl, CC0 1.0): the surface slaps
  (footstep_*), thuds (impactSoft_*), punches (impactPunch_*), bells
  (impactBell_*), planks, wood, metal, tin and glass impacts. Renamed to the
  game's `slap_*`, `thud_*`, `punch_*`, `bell_*`, `plank_*`, `bounce_*`,
  `glass_*`, `tin_*` files under `public/audio/sfx/`.
- artisticdude, "RPG Sound Pack" (opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack,
  CC0): swings, giant thuds, coins, interface clicks, cartoon randoms,
  bubbles, burp, door, small wood. Converted from WAV to OGG.
- "Forest Ambience" (opengameart.org/content/forest-ambience, CC0): the
  outdoor bed, `public/audio/ambience/forest.mp3`, transcoded to 64 kbps.
